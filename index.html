Less paperwork. Fewer escapes.

BrewLens does two jobs for part makers. We annotate your drawings so inspection sheets and first-article reports are ready sooner, and we inspect parts with cameras so defects are caught before the customer finds them.

What we do

Two services. One goal: parts that pass.

Use either on its own. Both are offered as a small pilot first, so you can judge the result on your own parts.

Paper side

Drawing annotation

Send a drawing. We number every dimension, tolerance, GD&T frame and note, and return a characteristic list and a draft inspection sheet.

  • Ballooned drawing in PDF
  • Inspection sheet or AS9102 forms
  • Readings mapped to each balloon
See how it works
Shop-floor side

Part inspection

A camera rig views each face of the part at a set angle, the same way every time, and flags surface defects for your inspector to confirm.

  • Repeatable views of every face
  • Images stored per part and serial
  • Your inspector makes the final call
