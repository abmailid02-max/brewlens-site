How it works

Position, capture, check, record.

1 · Position

Fixed views

A turntable selects the face. A tilting camera sets the elevation. Markers on the fixture confirm the pose actually reached.

2 · Capture

Same light, same distance

Controlled lighting and standoff, so differences between images come from the part and not the setup.

3 · Check

Flag, don't decide

Images are compared against good reference parts. Anything unusual is flagged for review.

4 · Record

Traceable

Each image is stored with part number, serial, face, time and result, ready for audits and customer complaints.

What we look for

Surface and visible features.

Best on machined and moulded parts where the defect is visible from outside. Defects inside the part, such as porosity, need X-ray and are not covered.

  • Scratches, dents and tool marks
  • Burrs and unbroken edges
  • Missing or incomplete features (holes, threads, chamfers)
  • Stains, rust and plating defects
  • Flash and short shots on moulded parts
  • Wrong part or wrong orientation
